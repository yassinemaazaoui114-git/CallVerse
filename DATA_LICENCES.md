  — https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce
- **Licence:** Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International (**CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**)
  — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/
- **Credit line:** "Contains data from the Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist (Kaggle), licensed under
  CC BY-NC-SA 4.0."
- **Where it is used:** ShopNova's real orders (task T15). The raw files are downloaded by hand into
  `data/raw/olist/` and are **not** in git. The processed sample in `callverse/data/real/`
  (`shopnova_olist_sample.json`, built by `scripts/build_shopnova_sample.py`) is **derived from Olist and keeps the
  same licence (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)**.
- **Changes made:** only electronics orders, 2,000 orders sampled, dates moved to 2026, prices converted from BRL to
  TND, statuses mapped, review texts removed; client names, emails, phones, product names, stock, carriers, tracking
  numbers and refunds are generated (see `docs/platform_contract.md` §2).
- **Use:** non-commercial university use only (CallVerse, a university data-science project).

## Hotel Booking Demand
- **Source:** Nuno Antonio, Ana de Almeida, Luis Nunes (2019), "Hotel booking demand datasets", *Data in Brief* 22,
  41–49, DOI [10.1016/j.dib.2018.11.126](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2018.11.126); file taken from Kaggle
  `jessemostipak/hotel-booking-demand`.
- **Licence:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (**CC BY 4.0**)
  — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- **Credit line:** "Contains data from Hotel Booking Demand (Antonio, Almeida, Nunes, 2019), licensed under CC BY 4.0."
- **Where it is used:** Atlas Travel's hotel bookings (task T20). The raw file is downloaded by hand into
  `data/raw/atlas/` and is **not** in git.
- **Taken:** 2,000 bookings drawn at random: hotel type, lead time, stay length, guests, meal, rooms reserved and
  assigned, deposit type, price per night, special requests, what happened (stayed, cancelled, no-show).
- **Changes made:** rows that cannot be a stay left out (0 nights, price of 0 or less, 0 adults); each booking moved to
  a new arrival date in 2026-07-11 → 2026-12-31 (lead time, stay length and days between cancelling and arrival
  kept); stays not finished on 2026-10-09 marked `confirmed`; EUR converted to TND at **3.358** (Wise, 2026-10-30);
  "Undefined" meal = SC, as the authors define it; deposit and status codes renamed; the two hotels given Atlas names
  and places (Lisbon, Algarve), which are fictional.

## 2015 Flight Delays and Cancellations
- **Source:** US Department of Transportation, Bureau of Transportation Statistics (US DOT / BTS); files taken from
  Kaggle `usdot/flight-delays`.
- **Licence:** **CC0** (public domain) — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- **Where it is used:** Atlas Travel's flights (task T20). The raw files are downloaded by hand into `data/raw/atlas/`
  and are **not** in git.
- **Taken:** 2,000 flights drawn at random: airline, flight number, route, distance, departure time, real delays,
  cancellation and its reason, delay causes; airline names and cities from `airlines.csv` and `airports.csv`.
- **Changes made:** rows left out: October 2015 (its numeric airport codes are not in `airports.csv`) and diverted
  flights (no arrival delay); each flight moved into the 60 days before 2026-10-09; miles converted to km; cause codes
  renamed.

## Generated by `scripts/build_atlas_sample.py` (Atlas Travel, not from any dataset)
Labelled `ai_generated = 1`: all user names, emails, phones and cities; all flight bookings; the refunds; the hotel
names. Flight bookings: lead time drawn from the real hotel lead times; price = **$377** (BTS, 2015 average domestic
fare) × (distance ÷ the sample's average distance), kept between 0.5× and 2.5×, × **3.005** TND per USD (Wise,
2026-10-30). The processed sample `callverse/data/real/atlas_sample.json` keeps the CC BY 4.0 credit above.

## ABCD — Action-Based Conversations Dataset (benchmark only, task T23)
- **Source:** Derek Chen, Howard Chen, Yi Yang, Alexander Lin, Zhou Yu (2021), "Action-Based Conversations Dataset:
  A Corpus for Building More In-Depth Task-Oriented Dialogue Systems", *NAACL 2021*; ASAPP —
  https://github.com/asappresearch/abcd (file `data/abcd_v1.1.json.gz`).
- **Licence:** **MIT** — https://github.com/asappresearch/abcd/blob/master/LICENSE
- **Credit line:** "Contains data from ABCD (ASAPP, Chen et al., NAACL 2021), licensed under MIT."
- **Where it is used:** only to benchmark M3 for ShopNova's Intake reasons (`benchmarks/m3/`). Not used by the running
  system. The raw file is put by hand into `data/raw/abcd/` and is **not** in git.
- **Taken:** for each chat of the `dev` split (validation) and, once at the end, of the `test` split (locked test):
  the first customer message with at least 4 words and the chat's `subflow` label.
- **Changes made:** subflows mapped to ShopNova reasons by `benchmarks/m3/data/abcd_mapping.json` (every other
  subflow = `other`, sampled to 150 per split, seed 2026). The validation file
  `benchmarks/m3/data/shopnova_intake_val.json` (committed, 328 messages) is derived from ABCD and keeps its MIT licence;
  the test file is only built into git-ignored `out/`.

## Data used to train the M3 LoRA adapter (T25b, adapter not adopted)
- **ABCD train** (MIT): fine-tuning of bge-small / bge-base (T25) and the adapter (T25b). Models stay in `models/` (git-ignored).
- **Bitext Retail + Bitext Travel customer-support datasets** (CDLA-Sharing 1.0, synthetic): training of the LoRA adapter
  only; no Bitext text is stored in this repository except the label mapping `benchmarks/m3/data/bitext_mapping.json`.
